How long will your download take?

Enter the actual download size and a measured connection speed. This planner works offline and keeps your inputs on the page.

Decimal GB × 8,000 ÷ Mbps gives seconds. For example, 100 GB at 100 Mbps is about 2 hours 14 minutes. That example is not a claim about GTA VI’s download size. Installation, verification, updates, pauses and changing speeds can add time.
